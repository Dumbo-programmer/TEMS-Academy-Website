Who we are

TEMS Academy of Olympiad Math & Science is based at House 06, Road 1/1, Shekertek Adabor, Mohammadpur-1207, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

For privacy questions or requests, write to temsonline3.14@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send an application or enquiry, we collect the student’s name, guardian email address, WhatsApp number, class, subject track, past contest experience if you choose to provide it, any message you write, and your consent confirmation.

The hosting provider processes technical request data, including an IP address, to deliver the site and limit abusive form submissions. We do not run advertising cookies, behavioural analytics or cross-site tracking.

Why we collect it

We use application details to reply, arrange a diagnostic, discuss placement, and manage admission. We rely on your consent and the need to respond to your request.

We do not sell personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

Who processes it

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Application emails are delivered through an email service provider (Resend) to the academy’s inbox. These providers process data on our behalf to deliver the service.

Only academy staff who need the information for admissions or teaching will see it.

Children’s information

Applications must be submitted by a parent or guardian. We ask for guardian consent and do not intentionally collect personal information directly from a child through this site.

How long we keep it

We keep application and admissions information only as long as needed to respond, arrange placement and maintain academy records. If you want an application removed, email us and we will delete it unless we are required to keep a record.

Security

The site uses HTTPS, server-side validation and rate limiting. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information you send.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the information you sent, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email temsonline3.14@gmail.com and describe the request.

You can also choose to contact the academy on WhatsApp or by email instead of using the form.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of the page will change. Material changes affecting applications will be described on this page.
