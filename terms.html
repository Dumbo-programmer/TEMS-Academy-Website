Information and results

We publish results, fees and batch details only when the academy has supplied or confirmed them. Where a detail is not confirmed, the site says so rather than filling the gap with a guess.

Competition results depend on the official organiser. TEMS does not guarantee selection for a national team, a medal or a particular competition outcome.

Applications and placement

An application is a request for a diagnostic and a placement conversation. It is not an offer of a place and does not create a contract.

Placement is based on the diagnostic and the academy’s judgement of the appropriate division and tier. The batch, session and fee are confirmed in writing before the first class.

Fees

The published monthly fee is Tk 2,500 for Mathematics and Tk 2,700 for Science. The fee does not change when a student is promoted to a higher tier.

The payment method and billing date are confirmed at placement. Scholarships and cash prizes are awarded separately at the Chairman’s discretion and are not a standard discount.

Attendance, changes and refunds

Batch changes, attendance rules and any refund arrangement are confirmed in the academy’s enrolment terms at placement. This website does not publish a refund policy.

If a batch is on hold or an intake changes, the academy will offer the running alternative or the next available intake.

Content and photographs

The text, design and original problems on this site belong to TEMS Academy. Photographs are used with the academy’s permission. Do not republish them without written consent.

You may quote a short passage with attribution and a link to this site.

External links

Links to olympiad organisers and other external sites are provided for reference. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or availability.
