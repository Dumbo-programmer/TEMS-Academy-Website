Subjects

Who teaches what.

Five teaching areas. The subject focus is published; individual biographies are not yet.

01

Mathematics

Algebra, geometry, number theory, combinatorics and proof.

Mentors work through problems alongside students, then read and mark written solutions. The emphasis is on a clear argument, not only a correct answer.

02

Physics

Mechanics, waves, electricity, thermodynamics and experimental work.

Sessions move between diagrams, assumptions and equations. Laboratory work connects the model to a measurement and its uncertainty.

03

Chemistry

Physical, inorganic and organic chemistry, with laboratory technique.

Students learn to move between mass, moles, concentration and structure, and to check a quantitative answer against the reaction it describes.

04

Biology

Cell biology, genetics, physiology, ecology and data interpretation.

Mentors push students from recall into structure–function reasoning and evidence-based explanation, the style IBO and IJSO reward.

05

Junior science

Integrated IJSO preparation across physics, chemistry and biology.

Junior science mentors connect the three sciences around a single problem, including practical work and experimental error.

In class

What the teaching team does.

The teaching model follows the academy’s published approach. Individual names, subjects and biographies are published only when the academy supplies and approves them.

  • Mentors work through problems alongside students rather than only lecturing from a textbook.
  • Written solutions are read and marked for the argument, not only the final answer.
  • Weekly problem sets and the monthly benchmark show when a placement should change.
  • The batch, session and fee are confirmed at placement; ask who will teach the group.

Publication policy

  • Names, subjects and credentials are published only with the mentor’s consent.
  • No profile, qualification or competition record is invented or borrowed.
  • The academy’s published material describes its mentors as Olympiad medalists, national-team alumni and senior academic faculty; individual claims will be added only when verified.
  • Mentor assignments and batch teaching teams are confirmed at placement.
