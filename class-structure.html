Placement

How placement works — in four moves.

A diagnostic test, a written placement, then a monthly review. Class in school does not decide the tier.

  1. 01

    Diagnostic test

    The student sits a short written diagnostic. It is a placement test, not a pass/fail entrance exam.

  2. 02

    Ability, not grade

    The result places the student in a division and tier. A strong Class 6 student can sit in a Junior Sigma batch; a Class 9 student may start in a foundation group.

  3. 03

    Written confirmation

    The batch, session and fee are confirmed in writing before the first class.

  4. 04

    Monthly benchmark

    Weekly problem sets build to a monthly benchmark. The top quartile may move up; a student who is slipping is re-placed.

How review works

  • Weekly problem sets are marked and returned with feedback.
  • A monthly benchmark checks whether the current tier still fits.
  • The top quartile of a batch may be promoted at the review.
  • A student who is slipping is re-placed rather than left behind.
  • Acceleration can happen at any time, not only at the monthly review.

Promotion path

Γ to Α to Σ — when the evidence says so.

Gamma builds the tools, Alpha applies them, Sigma stretches them. Movement is evidence-led and can happen in either direction.

  • Promotion is based on the monthly benchmark, not on time served.
  • The top quartile of a batch may move up at the monthly review.
  • A student who is slipping is re-placed into a group where the work fits.
  • Acceleration is available at any point when the evidence supports it.
  • The monthly fee stays the same after promotion.

Promotion is not automatic with age or time served. The monthly benchmark is the common review point; acceleration can happen sooner.
