Biology

A simple cross

Γ Gamma
In a species where AAA is dominant over aaa, a plant of genotype AAAAAA is crossed with a plant of genotype aaaaaa. What genotypes will the offspring have?
Hint
Each parent contributes one allele.
Worked solution
The AAAAAA parent can contribute only AAA, and the aaaaaa parent can contribute only aaa. Every offspring therefore receives AAA from one parent and aaa from the other, giving genotype AaAaAa.

Answer: All AaAaAa

Biology

Universal donor

Γ Gamma
Which ABO/Rh blood type is called the universal donor for red blood cells?
Hint
It has neither A nor B antigens and no Rh D antigen.
Worked solution
O negative blood has no A, B or Rh D antigens on the red cells, so it can be given to recipients of any ABO/Rh type in an emergency. It is called the universal donor type.

Answer: O negative
