The process

Four steps from question to first class.

The application is a request for a diagnostic and a placement conversation. It is not an offer of a place.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Send the application form on this site or message the academy on WhatsApp with the student’s name, guardian email, WhatsApp number, class, track and any past contest experience.

  2. 02

    Diagnostic

    The academy arranges a short written diagnostic. It is a placement test, not a pass/fail entrance exam. The length and format are confirmed when the test is arranged.

  3. 03

    Placement confirmed in writing

    The division, tier, session and monthly fee are confirmed in writing before the first class. If a batch is on hold, the academy will offer the running alternative or the next intake.

  4. 04

    First class

    The student attends the first class and begins the weekly problem sets. The first monthly benchmark follows at the end of the month.

About the diagnostic

The diagnostic tells us what the student can do now. It is not a test to cram for.

  • It places the student by ability, not by school class.
  • A strong student can be placed above their school grade; a student who needs consolidation can be placed below it.
  • The result is a division and tier: Primary, Junior or Secondary, and Gamma, Alpha or Sigma.
  • The placement is reviewed against a monthly benchmark and can change.

What to bring

  • The student’s current class and school details.
  • A guardian email address and WhatsApp number.
  • Any past olympiad results or certificates you want considered (optional).
  • A notebook and pen for the diagnostic.
  • The written placement confirmation when attending the first class.

The academy has not published a longer document checklist. Ask if you need a specific document before the diagnostic.

The first month

What happens, and when.

A clear sequence, so parents know what is happening and when the placement will be reviewed.

  1. Before class

    Application, diagnostic, written placement

    The batch, session and fee are confirmed before the student attends.

  2. Week 1

    First class and baseline problem set

    The mentor sees how the student works, not just what they already know.

  3. Weeks 2–3

    Weekly problem sets and feedback

    Sets are marked and returned. Mistakes are used as teaching material.

  4. Week 4

    First monthly benchmark

    The benchmark checks whether the placement still fits. The top quartile may move up; a student who is slipping is re-placed.

Ask a question
Which classes does TEMS teach?

TEMS runs offline batches for Classes 3–10 at its Dhaka campus, across Primary (3–5), Junior (6–8) and Secondary (9–10) divisions. The application form also accepts Classes 11–12 enquiries, but the academy has not confirmed whether it runs regular batches for those classes. Ask before applying.

Where is the campus?

House 06, Road 1/1, Shekertek Adabor, Mohammadpur-1207, Dhaka. The map pin is on the Contact page. Message on WhatsApp before visiting so a mentor can be free to meet you.

How much does it cost?

Mathematics is Tk 2,500 per month at any division and any tier. Science is Tk 2,700 per month and includes Physics, Chemistry, Biology and laboratory work. The fee does not change when a student is promoted.

Is there an entrance exam?

There is a diagnostic test, but it is not a pass/fail entrance exam. It places the student in the division and tier that fit their current ability. The placement is confirmed in writing before the first class.

My child is strong in school. Will the work be hard enough?

Placement ignores the school report. A student who is ready for harder problems is placed in a higher tier, including Sigma. The monthly benchmark then checks whether the tier is still the right fit.

Can my child move up or down?

Yes. The top quartile of a batch may be promoted at the monthly review, and a student who is slipping is re-placed rather than left behind. Acceleration can happen at any time when the evidence supports it.

What happens in the first month?

The student sits the diagnostic, receives a written placement, starts the weekly problem sets, and sits the first monthly benchmark at the end of the month. The benchmark decides whether the placement continues or changes.

Do you prepare for BdMO and international olympiads?

Yes. TEMS preparation follows the national pathway in each subject and the international competitions that pathway feeds, including BdMO, IJSO, IMSO, IPhO, APhO, IChO and IBO. The Pathway page shows each route with official links.

Are science laboratories included?

Science students work in the academy’s laboratories. The science programme covers Physics, Chemistry and Biology, including practical work relevant to IJSO, IPhO, IChO and IBO preparation.

Are scholarships available?

Merit scholarships and cash prizes are awarded separately at the Chairman’s discretion. They are not a standard discount on the monthly fee, and individual amounts are not published.

How do I apply?

Send the application form on this site or message the academy on WhatsApp. The process is: application, diagnostic test, placement confirmed in writing, then the first class. Response times vary with the admissions queue.

Can we visit before applying?

Yes, but message first. The academy has not published fixed office hours, so WhatsApp is the reliable way to arrange a visit.
