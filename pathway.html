Olympiad pathway

From a school round to an international team.

Bangladesh’s olympiad routes differ by subject. These timelines show the usual progression, the official organisers to follow, and where TEMS preparation fits. Always confirm dates and eligibility with the official source.

By subject

Five routes, one official source each.

Each subject has its own national route. The stages below are the usual progression; the official organiser controls registration, rounds and team selection. TEMS prepares students for the problem style at each stage but does not select national teams.

Mathematics

BdMO → national round → IMO and APMO

The Bangladesh Mathematical Olympiad is the main national route, with regional rounds feeding a national stage. The strongest students may be invited to selection camps for the IMO, and Bangladesh also competes in the Asia-Pacific Mathematics Olympiad.

The usual progression

  1. 01

    School & regional

    The BdMO season runs from December to February. School and regional rounds are the usual entry point; the official site publishes the current registration route for each class.

  2. 02

    National

    Regional winners and high scorers progress to the national round. The BdMO is open to students from Class I to Class XII, so the national stage spans primary through higher secondary.

  3. 03

    Selection

    National winners and awardees may be invited to training and selection camps for international teams. Selection is controlled by the organiser, not by any coaching academy.

  4. 04

    International

    Bangladesh sends a team to the International Mathematical Olympiad and competes in the Asia-Pacific Mathematics Olympiad. Both are proof-based competitions.

International routes

IMO International Mathematical Olympiad

Six problems over two days. National teams are selected through each country’s official route.

Eligibility Under 20 and not enrolled in tertiary education.

APMO Asia-Pacific Mathematics Olympiad

Five problems in four hours. Bangladesh competes in the Asia-Pacific region.

Physics

BdPhO → national selection → IPhO and APhO

The Bangladesh Physics Olympiad is the national route. It feeds selection for the International Physics Olympiad and the Asian Physics Olympiad. Both international competitions test theory and experimental work.

The usual progression

  1. 01

    School & regional

    BdPhO publishes its current registration and round structure each season. School and regional rounds are the usual first stage.

  2. 02

    National

    Successful students progress to the national BdPhO round, where problems require physical modelling rather than recall.

  3. 03

    Selection

    National performers may be invited to team selection and training. The BdPhO organiser controls the process.

  4. 04

    International

    Bangladesh sends a team to the International Physics Olympiad and competes in the Asian Physics Olympiad.

International routes

IPhO International Physics Olympiad

A theoretical and an experimental paper. Each national team is limited to five students, with two leaders.

Eligibility Secondary school students selected by their national organisation.

Official site ↗
APhO Asian Physics Olympiad

A regional physics competition with up to eight contestants per participating country.

Eligibility Secondary school students from participating Asian countries.

Chemistry

BChO → national selection → IChO

The Bangladesh Chemistry Olympiad is run through the Bangladesh Chemical Society. Strong national performers may be selected for the International Chemistry Olympiad, which combines theory with practical chemistry.

The usual progression

  1. 01

    School & regional

    The Bangladesh Chemical Society publishes the current BChO registration route and round structure. School and regional stages are the usual entry point.

  2. 02

    National

    The national BChO round tests inorganic, organic and physical chemistry beyond the school syllabus.

  3. 03

    Selection

    National awardees may be invited to selection and training for the international team. The chemical society controls selection.

  4. 04

    International

    Bangladesh participates in the International Chemistry Olympiad, which includes theoretical and practical examinations.

International routes

IChO International Chemistry Olympiad

The official steering committee site lists the current host, programme and regulations.

Eligibility Secondary school students selected by their national organisation.

Biology

BDBO → national selection → IBO

The Bangladesh Biology Olympiad is the national route to the International Biology Olympiad. IBO preparation rewards careful data interpretation and structure–function reasoning as much as factual recall.

The usual progression

  1. 01

    School & regional

    BDBO publishes its registration and round structure each season. School and regional rounds are the usual first stage.

  2. 02

    National

    The national BDBO round tests cell biology, genetics, physiology, ecology and evolution beyond the textbook.

  3. 03

    Selection

    National awardees may be invited to selection and training. The BDBO organiser controls the process.

  4. 04

    International

    Bangladesh participates in the International Biology Olympiad, which combines theoretical and practical tasks.

International routes

Eligibility Secondary school students selected by their national organisation.

Junior science

BdJSO → national camp → IJSO · IMSO

Junior science has two international routes. The Bangladesh Junior Science Olympiad feeds a six-member IJSO team through registration, regional or online rounds, a national round and a national camp. IMSO is the younger international route for mathematics and science.

The usual progression

  1. 01

    Registration

    Students register through the Bangladesh Junior Science Olympiad. The official route begins with registration, followed by regional or online rounds.

  2. 02

    Regional or online

    The first competitive stage is a regional or online round, depending on the cycle. The BdJSO publishes the format.

  3. 03

    National & camp

    Successful students progress to the national round and then a national camp. The camp prepares the team for the international competition.

  4. 04

    International

    Bangladesh sends a six-member team to the International Junior Science Olympiad. The 2026 IJSO is in Bulgaria. IMSO is a separate international route for younger students.

International routes

IJSO International Junior Science Olympiad

Integrated science: physics, chemistry and biology. Bangladesh’s route runs through BdJSO and a national camp.

Eligibility Under 16 on the competition’s cut-off date; confirm the current rule with the organiser.

Official site ↗
IMSO International Mathematics and Science Olympiad

A mathematics and science competition for younger students, with national delegations.

Eligibility Under 13; confirm the current rule with the organiser.
